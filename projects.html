PROJECT 01

Automated Microscopic Flaw Inspection System

1st Place, Capstone

Senior capstone sponsored by Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory: a start-to-finish product development effort for an automated system that inspects parts for microscopic flaws, in support of fusion research technologies. As project manager of a four-person team, I worked directly with the sponsors to define requirements and led the team through design reviews, while tracking budget and cost throughout the project. The system took first place among the capstone projects.

Machine Design · CAD · 2024

PROJECT 02

4 Wheel Drive RC Car

1st Place, Machine Design

A four-wheel-drive RC car designed and built for the Machine Design course. The frame is a complex-geometry part 3D printed in carbon fiber, paired with a custom drivetrain that integrates off-the-shelf components. Parts were produced using advanced manufacturing processes in a professional workshop, and the whole design was documented and evaluated in a comprehensive design report. The car won first place in the class.

Highlights

  • Complex-geometry 3D printed carbon fiber frame
  • Custom drivetrain integrated with off-the-shelf components
  • Detailed design and evaluation in a comprehensive report
  • Carbon fiber 3D printing
  • Drivetrain design
  • Advanced manufacturing
  • Design report
