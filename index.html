About me

Designer. Builder. Problem solver.

I graduated from Chico State University with a Bachelor of Science in Mechanical Engineering, Magna Cum Laude, with a 3.76 GPA, and was inducted into Tau Beta Pi, the Engineering Honor Society. I grew up in Redwood City, in the middle of the Bay Area and Silicon Valley, surrounded by technology and engineering. My father holds a PhD in aerospace engineering, and that environment shaped how I see the world.

I have always had a lot of ideas. As a kid I designed things out of whatever was lying around the house, and my passion for design and technical analysis has only grown since. In school that turned into first-place finishes in both Machine Design and Capstone, where I served as project manager on an automated flaw inspection system sponsored by Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory.

Outside the classroom I led the gearbox sub-team for Chico State's SAE Baja car, and spent a summer as a project engineering intern with Southland Industries on the largest healthcare construction project in California. Both taught me how real engineering gets done: many teams, many companies, and one finished product.

I love space and cars, and I want both to be part of my professional and personal life. Right now I am rebuilding my 2019 VW Jetta as a project car, and I enjoy getting my hands dirty. I am also an avid hiker and camper, and I like to travel and see the beauty the world has to offer.
